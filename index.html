Step 1: Specifications & Budget

Copy these rules into your engineering notebook before you start designing.

📏 The gap: 30 cm

Your bridge must cross a gap of 30 centimetres.

⭐ Golden Rule — free support

The bridge must rest freely on the gap. Taping, gluing or fixing the ends of the bridge to the desks is strictly forbidden. It has to hold itself up!

💰 Maximum budget: 10,000 credits

If your design costs more than this, the design is not viable.

📄 Paper: 1,000 credits per A4 sheet

Every sheet you use counts — including the ones you cut up.

🎗️ Tape: 100 credits per 10 cm strip

Tape is only for joining pieces (joints). You may not wrap or laminate the paper with tape.

Step 3: The Final Blueprint (technical drawing)

Choose your best idea and produce the Manufacturing Plan. It must be so clear that someone else could build your bridge without asking you a single question.

Draw your blueprint by hand Use an A4 sheet, a pencil and a ruler. Draw your bridge to scale (for example, 1 cm on paper = 1 cm in real life) using only straight lines. Write the length, height and width next to the drawing, and label every piece with what it is and what it is made of (e.g. "Pier: paper cylinder", "Deck: zigzag paper"). When you finish, take a sharp, well-lit photo of the sheet and upload that photo to Toddle. The on-screen canvas below is only for quick practice — the official blueprint must be hand-drawn on paper.
